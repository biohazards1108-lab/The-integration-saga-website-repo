FACTION DATABASE

The following factions played major roles during the Z‑Virus collapse and the subsequent Integration Era. Files remain incomplete — proceed with caution.

> THE FROZEN REALM

A survivalist coalition formed in the northern quarantine zones. Known for harsh discipline, brutal winters, and unmatched adaptability.

> THE EDEN DIRECTORATE

The original government body behind Project Eden. Their motives remain unclear — some files suggest benevolence, others hint at catastrophic miscalculations.

> THE INTEGRATED

Individuals who survived Z‑Virus exposure with altered physiology. Feared, revered, misunderstood — their existence reshaped the world.

> THE OUTLANDER CLANS

Nomadic groups roaming the wastelands. Masters of scavenging, guerrilla tactics, and ancient survival traditions.
